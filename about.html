Who we are

Powering everyday spaces

Marsle Electronics supplies modular switches, sockets, fan regulators and lamp holders to households, electricians, retailers and building contractors.

Founder's Message

Built on trust, one connection at a time

When I started MARSLE, I had one simple goal: every home and every shop deserves electrical accessories that are safe, reliable and fairly priced.

A switch or a socket may look like a small thing, but it is something a family touches every single day. That is why we never compromise on quality, and why we treat every customer, from a homeowner buying one switch to a contractor ordering a full truckload, with the same honesty and care.

Thank you for trusting MARSLE. We are here to power your spaces, today and for many years to come.
